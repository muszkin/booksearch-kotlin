# D10 — Support only free open models
- Date, owner: 2026-09-12, muszkin
- Context and the options weighed: The configured OpenRouter model could be any available model or be restricted by licensing and price.
- Decision and why: The feature must allow use only of open, free models exposed through OpenRouter.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: The implementation needs a precise, live model eligibility rule because OpenRouter model availability and pricing change.
- Status: active
