# D14 — Contextual books remain deliverable
- Date, owner: 2026-09-12, muszkin
- Context and the options weighed: D12 proposed that a contextual book be added to the library but blocked from delivery to Kindle and PocketBook.
- Decision and why: A contextual book is a normal library item and must never have a delivery block. It may be sent to Kindle or PocketBook like any other library item.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: D12's delivery restriction is superseded; contextual-role metadata must not alter delivery eligibility.
- Status: active
