# D18 — Acquire contextual books as normal deliverable library items
- Date, owner: 2026-09-12, muszkin
- Context and the options weighed: D12 combined contextual-book acquisition with a delivery restriction, and D14 rejected that restriction.
- Decision and why: The user may use the existing search and download flow to acquire a contextual book. It must be an EPUB in Polish by the same author, is added to the normal library, and may be delivered to Kindle or PocketBook.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: No contextual-role metadata changes delivery eligibility. Non-EPUB, non-Polish, and different-author results are rejected for use as context.
- Status: active
