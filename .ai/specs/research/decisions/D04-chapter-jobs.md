# D04 — Translate as resumable chapter jobs
- Date, owner: 2026-09-12, muszkin
- Context and the options weighed: A book can be translated in one run or as smaller units.
- Decision and why: Translate chapter by chapter so progress is visible and work can resume after a failure.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: The design must preserve chapter order, source-to-output mapping, and consistent glossary use.
- Status: active
