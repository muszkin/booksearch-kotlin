# D02 — Translate downloaded EPUB into a separate Polish EPUB
- Date, owner: 2026-09-12, muszkin
- Context and the options weighed: The library contains EPUB, MOBI, and PDF files; output could be preview-only or a separate library file.
- Decision and why: The first version accepts only downloaded EPUB files and creates a separate Polish EPUB linked to the source. The result may be downloaded and sent to Kindle or PocketBook.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: MOBI and PDF input are out of scope until their extraction and layout quality are separately evaluated.
- Status: active
