# D08 — Keep editing, PDF OCR, and sharing out of version one
- Date, owner: 2026-09-12, muszkin
- Context and the options weighed: The feature could include an editor, PDF OCR, automatic series detection, and public sharing.
- Decision and why: The first version excludes an in-app translation editor, OCR for scanned PDFs, and public sharing. Automatic series detection may be considered later.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Any of these requires a superseding scope decision.
- Status: active
